Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

Steam Stats (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an open-source Steam companion dashboard for exploring public Steam profile and library data. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use this Steam Stats instance, why, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. The operator of this instance is the party responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here.

2. The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, emails, or passwords.
  • No cookies, no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for profiling.
  • We handle the Steam identifier you search for, the public Steam data it returns, and standard technical data (like IP addresses) needed to run and protect the service.
  • Steam Stats is not directed to children under 13.

3. Information we handle

  • Steam identifiers you enter. When you search, you enter a SteamID64, profile URL, or vanity name. That input is sent to our backend so we can resolve it to a SteamID64 and load profile and library data.
  • Public Steam profile and library data. Information Steam makes available for a profile under that profile’s Steam privacy settings, such as persona name, avatar, profile URL, owned games, playtime, and recently played games, along with public game store details. This is fetched from the Steam Web API and Steam Store by our backend and displayed in the app.
  • Technical and log data. Like most websites, our servers automatically receive your IP address and basic browser information with each request. The backend logs your IP address, the date and time, the API address requested (which can include a SteamID64), and the response status. This is used for security, rate limiting, and troubleshooting, and is deleted after at most 9 days.
  • Data stored in your browser. The app stores a small amount of data in your browser’s localStorage. See Browser storage and cookies below.

4. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, payment information, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, or Steam password. We do not use cookies, advertising networks, social media pixels, or third-party analytics. Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from third-party font services. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. How information is used

  • To resolve Steam identifiers and display profile, library, statistics, and related dashboard features you request.
  • To remember the active profile in your browser so you do not have to re-enter it.
  • To cache responses so the service is fast and stays within Steam API usage limits.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the service, including rate limiting, preventing abuse, and diagnosing errors.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not use information for advertising or sell it to anyone.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Swiss data protection law applies to you, we rely on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in providing the dashboard you request, keeping it secure and reliable, and preventing abuse. We have balanced these interests against your rights and limited processing to public Steam data and the minimum technical data needed. Where we must keep or disclose information to meet a legal requirement, we rely on legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). You can object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time (see Your rights).

7. Browser storage and cookies

Steam Stats does not set cookies. It uses localStorage only for the functionality you request. These items are strictly necessary for the features you use, stay on your device, and are not used for tracking:

Key Purpose Kept until
steamstats.steamId Remembers the active SteamID64 between visits. You clear it in Settings or clear site data.
steamstats.gameDetails Caches public store details for up to 50 games. Entries expire after 2 days.
steamstats.catalogHints Caches public game artwork, genres, and platforms for library views. Entries expire after 2 days.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing site data for this website in your browser. Clearing the active profile in Settings removes the stored SteamID64.

8. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Server-side caches expire automatically:

  • Profile summaries: about 15 minutes.
  • Recently played games: about 5 minutes.
  • Owned games and playtime: about 1 hour.
  • Resolved Steam identifiers (vanity name or URL to SteamID64): about 1 day.
  • Public game store details: about 2 days.
  • Server request logs: at most 9 days. Logs are written to one file per day and deleted automatically once they are 9 days old. The only exception is if specific entries are needed to investigate abuse or meet a legal obligation; then we keep only those entries, and only for as long as necessary.

Cached data may be written to the server’s disk so it survives restarts, but it still expires on the schedule above, and caches may be cleared earlier. The web server does not keep an access log.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising. Information is disclosed only as follows:

Your browser also loads game artwork and avatars directly from Steam’s content delivery networks, which means those servers receive your IP address and standard request data. Valve’s handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy. Steam Stats is not affiliated with Valve Corporation.
  • Hosting and network providers. This instance may run on third-party hosting and use network, CDN, or security providers (such as Cloudflare) that process technical data like IP addresses on our behalf to deliver and protect the site.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or the service.
  • Project changes. If the project changes maintainers or operators, information we hold may transfer to the new operator, who will remain bound by this policy or notify you of changes.

Links to external sites (such as Steam or GitHub) take you to services with their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

10. If your profile is looked up

Anyone can enter a Steam identifier, so Steam Stats may display information about Steam users who have never visited the site. We only show what Steam makes public for that profile, and we do not combine it with other sources. If you do not want your information shown, set your Steam profile or game details to private in Steam’s privacy settings. Once you do, Steam stops returning that data and our cached copy expires on the schedule above. You can also contact us to ask for cached data about your SteamID64 to be removed sooner.

Steam Stats is a general-audience service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information, including persistent identifiers such as IP addresses or Steam IDs, from children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to use the service. Steam itself requires users to be at least 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it as quickly as reasonably possible. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used Steam Stats or that we hold information about your child, contact us and we will delete what we can identify. Parents can also clear the browser data described above and set their child’s Steam profile to private. Where local laws set a higher age for consent to data processing (for example, up to 16 in parts of the EU), users below that age should only use Steam Stats with parental permission.
